My Strengths

Above all, I am an artist. 2D pixel art is what I specialize in but I am also capable of creating digital art, and some basic 3D models such as household objects I love character and environment design, but am less skilled at UI design and background design.

I am less confident in my programming abilities, but I have been able to create some impressive blueprints from scratch. For example, the behaviour tree for the enemies in Ballad of Brineshore, which are showcased in my showreel and my virtual gallery.
