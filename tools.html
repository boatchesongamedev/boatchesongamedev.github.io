Tools

For creating the games themselves, I use Unreal Engine 5 and utilise blueprints within the engine. I currently do not know any programming languages, but I plan on beginning to learn C++ next summer after this course has completed.

For pixel art and animation I use the browser based PISKEL, and the application Aseprite.

For 3D modelling I use Blender, and Adobe 3D Substance Painter for texturing. I am also able to texture 3D models within blender itself.

For video editing, I know how to use Adobe After Effects for compositions and Adobe Premiere Pro to edit them together.

For digital art and photo editing, I primarily use Krita, but have some knowledge of Adobe Photoshop.
