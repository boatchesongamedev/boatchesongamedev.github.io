About

Hey! My name is Bo Atcheson. I'm a Level 3 Games Design student at Belfast Metropolitan College, currently attending my second year. I'm passionate about creating video games as a hobby, particularly the artistic element of games development, but I hope to one day join a team of developers who are as enthusiastic as I am

I enjoy all sorts of genres of games, especially 2D plaformers and turn based RPGs. In terms of the artistic side of things, I have an interest in pixel art and low poly, DS era 3D graphics.

My next step in my creative journey is to finish my course at Belfast Met, ending the year having completed a demo for a sidescroller RPG set on the streets of Belfast. I look forward to being able to share it with you!
